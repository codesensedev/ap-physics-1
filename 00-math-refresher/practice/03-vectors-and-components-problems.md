# Practice · 03 · Vectors and components

★ = do these in the 30-minute session. Use **degree** mode on your calculator. Try each one yourself before you open the [solutions](03-vectors-and-components-solutions.md).

---

### 1 ★ *(Type A: resolve)*

A force of 20 N acts at 40° above the $+x$ axis. Find $F_x$ and $F_y$.

### 2 *(Type A: resolve, signs)*

A ball is kicked at 15 m/s, 60° above the horizontal, toward the **left** (the $-x$ direction). Find $v_x$ and $v_y$.

### 3 ★ *(Type A: angle from the vertical)*

A rope pulls with a force of 80 N at 25° **from the vertical**, tilted to the right. Find the horizontal and vertical components of the force.

### 4 *(Type B: rebuild)*

A vector has $A_x = 12$ and $A_y = -5$. Find its size and its direction.

### 5 *(Type B: rebuild, quadrant trap)*

A velocity has $v_x = -6.0$ m/s and $v_y = +8.0$ m/s. Find the speed and the direction. Give the direction as an angle measured counterclockwise from $+x$.

### 6 ★ *(Type C: add)*

$\vec A$ is 5.0 m long and points along $+x$. $\vec B$ is 7.0 m long and points 60° above $+x$. Find the size and direction of $\vec A + \vec B$.

### 7 *(Type C: subtract)*

$\vec A = (4,\ 3)$ and $\vec B = (1,\ 5)$. Find $\vec A - \vec B$, then its size and direction.

### 8 ★ *(Type D: qualitative)*

A vector of fixed size $A$ rotates from 0° (along $+x$) up to 90° (along $+y$).

(a) How do $A_x$ and $A_y$ change?
(b) At what angle are they equal?
(c) Can a component ever be larger than the vector itself? Explain.

### 9 *(Type D: qualitative)*

Two vectors have sizes 3 and 4.

(a) What is the largest possible size of their sum?
(b) What is the smallest?
(c) At what angle between them is the size of the sum exactly 5?
