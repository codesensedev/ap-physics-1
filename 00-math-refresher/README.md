# Unit 0 · Math refresher

Not part of the College Board's course, but every later unit leans on it. Five short lessons cover exactly the math that AP Physics 1 uses, and nothing else.

| # | Lesson | You'll be able to | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Algebra and units | rearrange an equation for any symbol; use units to catch mistakes | planned |
| 02 | Trig essentials | use sin, cos, tan in right triangles; read angle conventions | planned |
| 03 | [Vectors and components](03-vectors-and-components.md) | split a slanted vector into $x$ and $y$ parts; rebuild it; add vectors | ✅ written |
| 04 | Graphs: slope and area | find slope and area on a graph and say what they mean; straighten curves | planned |
| 05 | Ratios and scaling | answer "if this doubles, that becomes..." questions without a calculator | planned |

A short diagnostic (planned) will show which lessons can be skipped.

## You're ready for Unit 1 when you can

- rearrange $v = v_0 + at$ for $t$, then for $a$, without hesitation;
- find the side of a right triangle given the hypotenuse and an angle;
- split a 20 N force at 40° into horizontal and vertical parts;
- say what the slope and the area under a line on a velocity–time graph mean;
- say what happens to $y$ if $y \propto x^2$ and $x$ doubles.
