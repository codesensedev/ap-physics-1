# Roadmap

**Goal:** finish all new content with time left for mixed review and full practice exams before the AP Physics 1 exam on **Wednesday, May 5, 2027**.

**Plan basis:** 3 sessions a week × 30 minutes, starting the week of **Nov 2, 2026**. That is 26 weeks, about 78 sessions: 65 lessons plus about 13 review and practice-exam sessions.

## Reading the plan

- **Dates assume 3 sessions every week with no breaks.** Holidays and busy weeks will slip things; the 4th and 5th weekly sessions are the buffer.
- **At 4 sessions a week** the new content is done by about the end of February; **at 5**, by about the end of January. The extra time goes to review and practice exams, not to more content.
- **Every unit ends with a mixed-problem session** that combines it with earlier units. This is what keeps the course connected.
- **Unit 0 can be shortened.** A short diagnostic (planned) will show which of the five refresher lessons can be skipped.
- Confirm the exam date, format, and equation sheet against the College Board's current *Course and Exam Description* before the final review weeks.
